Ford's F-Series is the benchmark for trucks in Canada, and for good reason. Tough, smart, and versatile, Ford trucks are built to work as hard as you do.

The Ford F-150 has been Canada's best-selling truck for decades. Available in multiple cab configurations, box lengths, and powertrain options, including a V8, a turbocharged EcoBoost, and a hybrid option, the F-150 adapts just as well to everyday needs as it does to the most demanding job sites. The Pro Power Onboard technology turns your truck into a mobile power source, perfect for field workers and camping enthusiasts alike.

For those whose needs go beyond what a half-ton can offer, the Ford Super Duty series (F-250, F-350, and F-450) represents the elite of work trucks. With towing capacities exceeding 18,000 kg and impressive payload options, the Super Duty is the go-to choice for construction contractors, farmers, and anyone whose work tolerates no compromise. Available in regular cab, SuperCab, or Crew Cab configurations, with single or dual rear wheels, the Super Duty can be configured to your exact requirements.

Ford's SUV lineup is one of the most diverse on the Canadian market. From city driving to off-road adventure, each model has been designed with a specific driver in mind.

Compact, agile, and well-equipped, the Ford Escape is the ideal choice for urban drivers and small families. Available in hybrid and plug-in hybrid versions, the Escape delivers remarkable fuel efficiency without sacrificing comfort or connectivity. Its thoughtfully designed interior, driver assistance technologies, and city maneuverability make it one of the most popular compact SUVs in Canada.

The Ford Bronco Sport is built for those who want to venture off the beaten path without giving up everyday practicality. With available all-wheel drive, GOAT (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain) drive modes, and clever cargo space, the Bronco Sport is just as comfortable on a dirt trail as it is in a shopping centre parking lot.

The iconic Ford Bronco is back, and it hasn't lost any of its character. Built on a body-on-frame platform with solid axles and two-speed four-wheel drive, the Bronco is a true off-road beast capable of tackling the most challenging terrain. Available in two- or four-door versions, with a removable roof and countless accessory options, the Bronco is made for those who see the road as just a starting point.

The Ford Edge is aimed at drivers who want a mid-size SUV that blends style, space, and technology. With its refined interior, comfortable seating for five, and turbocharged powertrain, the Edge delivers a dynamic drive and everyday versatility. A solid choice for those who want more than a compact SUV without stepping up to a full-size.

Space, power, and versatility, the Ford Explorer is the ultimate family SUV. With three rows of seating, towing capacity of up to 2,268 kg, and a choice of powertrains including a hybrid option, the Explorer is ready for long family road trips and weekend getaways alike. Its advanced onboard technology and flexible seating configurations make it an indispensable travel companion.

When space and power are non-negotiable, the Ford Expedition delivers. This full-size three-row SUV can seat up to eight passengers while offering one of the highest towing capacities in its class, up to 4,173 kg. Available in the extended Expedition Max version for even more cargo space, the Expedition is the definitive choice for large families, group travel, and those who refuse to leave anything behind.

Somewhere between an SUV and a sports car, the Ford Mustang Mach-E is in a class of its own. Fully electric, it delivers impressive performance (the GT version can reach 100 km/h in under 4 seconds) combined with a range of up to 490 km depending on configuration. With its bold design, large vertical centre screen, and connected technologies, the Mach-E redefines what an electric SUV can be.

Ford has committed to an ambitious electric transition, and the results are already on our roads. In addition to the Mustang Mach-E, the F-150 Lightning brings the electric revolution to the world of work trucks. With an estimated range of up to 515 km, a towing capacity of up to 4,536 kg, and Ford Intelligent Backup Power technology that can power your home during an outage, the Lightning isn't just an electric truck, it's a major technological leap forward.

For drivers who want to explore electric without changing everything at once, Ford's hybrid and plug-in hybrid options (available on the Escape, Explorer, and F-150) offer a smooth transition toward more fuel-efficient driving.
